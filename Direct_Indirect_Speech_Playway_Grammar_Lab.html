1. Meet the Two Speakers! 👦👧

🎤

Direct Speech

We repeat the speaker's exact words. We use quotation marks (“ ”).

Ravi said, “I am happy.”

ஒருவர் பேசிய அதே சொற்களை மாற்றாமல் கூறுவது Direct Speech. மேற்கோள் குறிகள் (“ ”) பயன்படுத்தப்படும்.

🗨️

Indirect Speech

We report the speaker's words without quotation marks.

Ravi said that he was happy.

ஒருவர் கூறிய கருத்தை நம் சொற்களில் தெரிவிப்பது Indirect Speech. மேற்கோள் குறிகள் நீக்கப்படும்.

🎭 Mini Story

Ravi: “I am hungry.”
Meena: Ravi said that he was hungry.

ரவி நேரடியாகப் பேசும்போது Direct Speech. மீனா ரவி கூறியதைப் பிறரிடம் தெரிவிக்கும்போது Indirect Speech.

Remember!

  • Direct = Exact words.
  • Indirect = Reported meaning.
  • Quotation marks are removed in indirect speech.

2. The Change-O-Matic Machine 🔄

When the reporting verb is in the past (said/told) and the reporting situation requires a backshift, the tense commonly moves one step back.

Direct SpeechIndirect SpeechExample
Simple PresentSimple Past“I play.” → He said that he played.
Present ContinuousPast Continuous“I am playing.” → He said that he was playing.
Present PerfectPast Perfect“I have played.” → He said that he had played.
Simple PastPast Perfect“I played.” → He said that he had played.
WillWould“I will play.” → He said that he would play.
CanCould“I can swim.” → He said that he could swim.
MayMight“I may come.” → He said that he might come.

தமிழ் குறிப்பு: எல்லா இடங்களிலும் ஒரே மாதிரி மாற்றம் செய்யக் கூடாது. யார் பேசுகிறார், யாரிடம் பேசுகிறார், எப்போது/எங்கு பேசுகிறார் என்பதைக் கவனித்து Pronoun மற்றும் Time/Place சொற்களை மாற்ற வேண்டும்.

Important: Universal truths and facts that are still true generally remain in the present tense. Example: The teacher said, “The sun rises in the east.” → The teacher said that the sun rises in the east.

THE REPORTER RAP

🎤 Direct means exact words – quote them right!
🗣️ Indirect reports the meaning – take the quotes out of sight!
👏 Tense may move back – check the reporting time!
👏 Pronouns change with meaning – make the sentence fine!
👏 Now becomes then; today becomes that day!
👏 Tomorrow becomes the next day – hip, hip, hooray!

தமிழ் நினைவுக் குறிப்பு:
Direct – பேசிய அதே சொற்கள்.
Indirect – கூறிய செய்தியைத் தெரிவிப்பது.
Quotation marks நீக்கு; தேவையான இடத்தில் tense, pronoun, time/place சொற்களை மாற்று.
