1. Meet the Three Sentence Characters!

🧒

SIMPLE SENTENCE

One independent clause expressing a complete thought.

Ravi plays cricket.
ஒரு முழுமையான கருத்தைத் தரும் ஒரே Independent Clause கொண்ட வாக்கியம் Simple Sentence எனப்படும்.
🤝

COMPOUND SENTENCE

Two or more independent clauses joined together.

Ravi plays cricket, and Mani watches him.
இரண்டு அல்லது அதற்கு மேற்பட்ட தனித்து முழுமையான கருத்தைத் தரும் Independent Clauses இணைவது Compound Sentence ஆகும்.
🏰

COMPLEX SENTENCE

One independent clause and at least one dependent clause.

When school ends, the children go home.
ஒரு Independent Clause உடன் குறைந்தது ஒரு Dependent Clause இணைந்திருப்பது Complex Sentence ஆகும்.

😂 Funny Story: The Three Friends

Simple Sam: “I love mangoes.” 🥭
Compound Coco: “I love mangoes, and I eat them daily.” 😋
Complex Clara: “I eat mangoes because they are sweet.”
நினைவில் கொள்: Simple – ஒரு Independent Clause. Compound – இரண்டு அல்லது அதற்கு மேற்பட்ட Independent Clauses. Complex – ஒரு Independent Clause + குறைந்தது ஒரு Dependent Clause.
SIMPLE = 1 Independent Clause
COMPOUND = Independent + Independent
COMPLEX = Independent + Dependent

2. Sentence Detective – Find the Clauses! 🔍

Independent Clause

A clause that can stand alone and express a complete thought.

The bell rang. ✔

Dependent Clause

A clause that cannot normally stand alone as a complete sentence.

because the bell rang… ✖ (The thought is incomplete.)

5. 🏆 Public Examination Practice Zone

A. Identify the sentence type

  1. Being tired, he went to bed early.
  2. He worked hard, yet he failed.
  3. Although it was raining, we went to school.
  4. The sun rose and the birds began to sing.
  5. On seeing the police, the thief ran away.

B. Combine as directed

  1. He was ill. He attended the meeting. (Compound)
  2. Work hard. You will succeed. (Complex)
  3. He finished his homework. He went out to play. (Simple)
A1 Simple – one main clause with a phrase.
A2 Compound – two independent clauses joined by yet.
A3 Complex – although-clause is dependent.
A4 Compound – two independent clauses joined by and.
A5 Simple – one main clause with a phrase.

B1 He was ill, but he attended the meeting.
B2 If you work hard, you will succeed.
B3 Having finished his homework, he went out to play.
